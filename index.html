Understanding New Year time zones

There are more than 30 different local times on Earth, so New Year is celebrated in waves for about a full day. The Line Islands of Kiribati (UTC+14:00) are first, followed by Samoa, Tonga and New Zealand. Asia, Europe and Africa follow, and the Americas celebrate last, with American Samoa and Niue (UTC-11:00) among the very last inhabited places. In December it is summer in the Southern Hemisphere, so cities such as Sydney, Auckland and Santiago de Chile celebrate on daylight saving time.
